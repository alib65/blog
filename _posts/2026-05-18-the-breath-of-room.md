I am happy to share my new article, recently published in Metapsychosis Magazine.

In this article, I explored “The Breath of the Room,” a framework for my electroacoustic compositions that transforms spatial sound into a multi-sensory poetic sensation. Drawing on the phenomenology of Bachelard and Böhme, I detailed my Electropoem series, which utilizes multichannel arrays and microtonal systems to move away from linear time and toward an immersive “dream-place.” 

A huge thanks to my friend and wonderful writer, Michael Eisenberg, for his fantastic editorial work on this article.
