Excited to share that my piece "The Mute Choir of the Streets" is part of the Festival de Radioarte at POSVERSO 2026, the international biennial of experimental poetry taking place in Argentina this year.

The piece airs on POSVERSO Radio on:

- Tuesday, Sept 15
- Friday, Sept 18
- Monday, Sept 21
- Wednesday, Sept 23
- Friday, Sept 25
- Sunday, Sept 27
- Tuesday, Sept 29

alongside radio art works from artists across 11 countries.
