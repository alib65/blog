<p>The full version of <em>Micromorphosis No. 1</em> is now available on YouTube.</p>

<p>This electroacoustic audio-visual composition explores the application of microtonal modulation. The work utilizes a custom software architecture to map real-time spatial audio data onto 3D topographical models. This process establishes a direct correspondence where auditory parameters dictate the visual output.</p>

<strong>Micromorphosis No. 1 (2026)</strong><br>
Composer: <a href="http://www.alibalighi.com" target="_blank">Ali Balighi</a><br>
